Upon further thought; instead of removing factors to re-calculate factors (which seemed to fail in my example anyway due to 4, 6 and 10 all sharing factors), we can instead calculate the list of all the factors of the length of the number currently being checked. Then, we can go through that list, from lowest to highest, and check if that is a factor of any later numbers. If it is, we count how many times it is a factor. If it is just once, we can simply ignore the total as it is already calculated later. If it is included more than once, we can remove it's total for how many times over once it is a factor of another factor:

-Calculate factors of length
-For each, count how many times it is a factor of other factors.
-If it is not a factor of the other factors; add its total value to the total of invalid IDs.
-If it is a factor once, ignore it
-If it is a factor more than once, minus its total * the amount of times it is a factor more than once.
