The Triple Ds:

Decadence,
Victoria Cheese

The classic taste of a Victoria strawberry and vanilla,
Paired with a decadent dark chocolate twist; 
This D's rich and sweet tug of war,
Will leave you begging for more...

Dream,
Ruby Biscoff

The sensuous salty taste of caramel,
Combined with the luscious sweet burst of raspberry;
This D's tease of naughty and nice,
Won't have you putting down this slice...

Delight,
Dark Forest

The subtle sweet notes of blueberry;
The strong rich tones of blackberry;
This D's blend of juicy tarts,
Will have you puckering up tonight...
